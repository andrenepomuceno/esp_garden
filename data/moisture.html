Dry / Humid / Wet come from a Gaussian naive Bayes model fitted per probe, one Gaussian per class. The labels are not guessed from the shape of the data — they come from the watering record: soil is wettest shortly after its pump runs and driest just before the next time it does. Training runs once a day and accumulates, because one day holds only a watering cycle or two.
A probe reports nothing until its model earns it. That is deliberate: an earlier attempt to derive these bands by clustering a month of history produced three confident groups that were an artefact of the drying trend plus the readings of a disconnected probe.
